# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers own release readiness and coordinate the activities required to deliver a change safely to production.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm release scope, readiness criteria, approvals, and scheduling
- Coordinate rollback and mitigation plans
- Prepare release communications and stakeholder updates
- Track release dependencies and handoffs

### Goals
- Improve release predictability and reduce production risk
- Ensure teams have clear go/no-go criteria
- Make rollback and recovery actions understood before deployment

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager on timelines, dependencies, and stakeholder coordination.
- Works with the Product Manager to confirm scope and release value.
- Coordinates with Developers on deployment readiness and rollback mechanisms.
- Partners with QA/Testing on acceptance criteria, smoke testing, and sign-off.

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Facilitators help the delivery team follow agreed ways of working, improve flow, and remove impediments.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and remove delivery impediments
- Encourage healthy team collaboration and continuous improvement
- Coach teams on backlog readiness and workflow practices

### Goals
- Improve delivery flow and team effectiveness
- Reduce avoidable delays and recurring blockers
- Create a psychologically safe environment for feedback

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager on schedule health, dependencies, and escalation needs.
- Works with Developers and QA/Testing to improve daily workflow and remove blockers.
- Partners with the Product Manager to keep backlog items ready and outcome-focused.
- Supports stakeholders by making delivery constraints and decisions visible.

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads lead user research and experience decisions, turning customer needs into usable designs and validating proposed solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and test user flows, prototypes, and experience requirements
- Translate customer and stakeholder feedback into design direction
- Validate usability and accessibility considerations
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria from a user-experience perspective

### Goals
- Improve usability and customer satisfaction
- Reduce costly rework caused by unclear or unvalidated designs
- Ensure accessibility and consistent experience standards

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Product Manager to define the problem, desired outcomes, and solution trade-offs.
- Partners with Developers on design feasibility and implementation.
- Works with QA/Testing to validate experience and acceptance details.
- Gathers and communicates stakeholder and customer feedback.

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads define security requirements, advise on risk, and coordinate reviews and remediation throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Establish security requirements and review milestones
- Identify, assess, and track security risks
- Coordinate security validation and remediation
- Advise teams on secure design and implementation choices

### Goals
- Reduce security risk without blocking delivery unnecessarily
- Make security ownership and acceptance criteria explicit
- Improve incident and vulnerability readiness

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Product Manager on security trade-offs and prioritization.
- Partners with Developers on threat modeling, secure implementation, and remediation.
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on security tests and acceptance validation.
- Works with the Project Manager to track security risks and escalate material exposure.

---

## Site Reliability / Operations Lead

### Role Summary
Site Reliability or Operations Leads establish operational readiness, observability, capacity, and incident-response expectations for delivered services.

### Responsibilities
- Define reliability, availability, monitoring, and capacity requirements
- Coordinate deployment and service-run readiness
- Prepare incident response, escalation, and recovery practices
- Review operational risks and improve service observability

### Goals
- Improve service reliability and recovery speed
- Make production readiness measurable before release
- Reduce operational surprises after launch

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA/Testing on monitoring, production readiness, and validation.
- Partners with the Release Manager on deployments, rollback, and post-deploy verification.
- Works with the Project Manager to track operational risks and dependencies.
- Provides operational evidence to the Product Manager for prioritization and outcome decisions.

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts define, collect, validate, and interpret the measures used to evaluate project and product outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and measurement approaches
- Validate instrumentation and data quality
- Analyze outcomes and identify trends or risks
- Communicate findings and recommendations to stakeholders

### Goals
- Improve evidence-based prioritization and decision-making
- Establish reliable measures of customer and business outcomes
- Identify benefit or risk signals early

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Product Manager to define success metrics and interpret outcomes.
- Partners with Developers and QA/Testing on instrumentation and data validation.
- Shares findings with the Project Manager for planning and risk updates.
- Presents evidence and limitations to stakeholders in clear, actionable terms.

---

## Customer / Stakeholder Advocate

### Role Summary
Customer or Stakeholder Advocates represent customer needs and stakeholder context, bringing feedback and constraints into planning and prioritization.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and synthesize customer or stakeholder feedback
- Communicate needs, constraints, and trade-offs to the delivery team
- Validate that proposed solutions address the intended need
- Help prioritize engagement and communication for affected groups

### Goals
- Improve customer and stakeholder alignment
- Reduce the risk of building for assumed needs
- Make feedback and decision rationale visible

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Product Manager on outcomes, prioritization, and roadmap trade-offs.
- Works with the Project Manager on stakeholder communication and decisions.
- Partners with UX/Design, Developers, and QA/Testing during discovery and validation.
- Raises unresolved stakeholder concerns to the appropriate decision owner.
