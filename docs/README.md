# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README provides a single entry point to OctoAcme's project management guidance. The process is intentionally lightweight and iterative: teams validate the business need, stakeholders, success measures, and go/no-go decision during initiation; turn approved work into prioritized increments with clear acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, owners, and risk/dependency tracking during planning; execute through a project board, regular team and stakeholder updates, testing, review, and blocker escalation; perform documented readiness checks, deployment verification, and rollback planning for releases; and close with retrospectives and tracked improvement actions. Project Managers coordinate delivery, Product Managers own outcomes and prioritization, and developers, QA, and stakeholders collaborate on quality and decisions.
